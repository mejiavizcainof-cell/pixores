# Pixores backlink outreach sprint

Prepared: 2026-08-15

Primary linkable asset: https://www.pixores.com/research/image-format-timeline

## Positioning

Pixores is a browser-based image and creator toolkit. The outreach angle is not “please link to our converter.” It is a useful, independently understandable reference: a sourced timeline and downloadable dataset covering GIF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF.

Proof points available on the asset:

- Primary-source links for every standards milestone.
- A capability matrix for compression, transparency, animation, and typical use.
- A downloadable CSV dataset.
- A public methodology and citation format.
- Supporting deep-dive articles on PNG, GIF, JPEG, and WebP.

## Priority targets

### 1. SaaSHub product listing

- Submit: https://www.saashub.com/submit
- Fit: Pixores is a live web product with multiple image and creator workflows.
- Required preparation: product URL, logo, screenshots, pricing (“free web tools”), categories, and competitors.
- Suggested categories: Photos & Graphics, Design Tools, Online Services.

Short description:

> Pixores is a practical browser toolkit for resizing, converting, compressing, cropping, upscaling, and preparing images, plus creator workflows for thumbnails and video. Core web tools are free to use, and the Pixores editorial library publishes sourced visual-format research.

### 2. Product Hunt launch

- Official posting guide: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product
- Featuring criteria: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines
- Position the complete Pixores product, not an isolated report or simple converter.
- Prepare at least two 1270×760 gallery images and a short demo before scheduling.

Tagline:

> Convert, improve, and create visual content in one browser toolkit

Description under 260 characters:

> Pixores combines fast browser image tools with thumbnail and video workflows. Resize, convert, compress, crop, upscale, remove backgrounds, and use sourced visual-format guides without installing a traditional editing suite.

First-comment outline:

1. The problem: quick visual tasks are scattered across unrelated tools.
2. What Pixores combines today.
3. Privacy and processing behavior that can be verified on each tool page.
4. Why the historical-format research was published.
5. Ask for specific feedback on the workflow and reference dataset.

### 3. Smashing Magazine editorial pitch

- Guidelines: https://www.smashingmagazine.com/write-for-us/
- Editorial process: https://www.smashingmagazine.com/editorial-process/
- This must be a new, non-promotional article. Mention Pixores only as the source of the research experience or in the author biography if the editor permits it.

Pitch concept:

> What Thirty Years of Image Formats Teach Us About Web Performance Decisions

Outline:

1. Why format choice is a requirements decision, not a popularity contest.
2. GIF and the value of predictable behavior.
3. JPEG and perceptual compromise.
4. PNG and the cost of exactness.
5. WebP/AVIF and the operational cost of modern delivery.
6. A repeatable decision framework for designers and developers.

### 4. A List Apart editorial pitch

- Contribution guidelines: https://alistapart.com/about/contribute/
- Contact: https://alistapart.com/contact/
- Submit an original argument, not a product announcement.

Pitch concept:

> Image Formats Are Product Decisions: A Framework for Choosing What the Web Delivers

The argument should connect format selection to accessibility, editorial fidelity, performance budgets, and long-term maintenance. Use the Pixores dataset as supporting research only where editorial policy permits.

### 5. web.dev external-author pitch

- Contribution information: https://web.dev/about
- Pitch an evidence-backed article about measurable image-delivery decisions, not a Pixores feature tour.
- Suggested angle: a reproducible method for comparing output dimensions, visual error, decoding support, and transfer size.

## Community distribution after publication

- Publish a concise methodology walkthrough on DEV Community with a canonical link back to the full dataset where platform settings allow it.
- Share the research and ask for corrections in relevant web-performance or image-format communities; do not mass-post identical copy.
- Create one visual excerpt for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook, each pointing to the research page rather than the home page.
- Contact authors of current image-format explainers only when the Pixores dataset materially improves or updates a specific section of their article.

## Quality rules

- Personalize every message around a real paragraph or resource.
- Do not buy bulk links or use automated directory blasts.
- Do not request links from unrelated sites.
- Do not claim benchmark results that the public asset cannot reproduce.
- Track the destination, contact date, response, published URL, and referral traffic.
- Stop after one follow-up unless the recipient asks for more information.

## First 30-day target

- 2 product listings submitted manually.
- 3 editorial pitches tailored to each publication.
- 12 highly relevant author/resource-page contacts.
- 4 original social excerpts, not duplicate link drops.
- Goal: the first 3 legitimate referring domains and at least one editorial citation.
